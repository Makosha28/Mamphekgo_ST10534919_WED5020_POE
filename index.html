Our Purpose

Life is a rollercoaster. We can do all things through Christ who strengthens us — and your confidence is our victory.

Together with the provision of clothes, food and sanitary towels, we hug those who need them. Some wounds stay with you for a lifetime. The pain of not knowing what you are going to eat tomorrow kills passion and vision.

What you donate leaves a smile on the faces of those in need. We aim to build a society where every young person believes they can achieve whatever they want, free from limitations and fears.
