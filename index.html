The Woman Behind The Magic

The goal is to build confidence and help those in need.

Life is a rollercoaster. We can do all things through Christ who strengthens us — and your confidence is our victory.

The pain of not having someone who hugs you when you get home becomes a wound. Together with the provision of clothes, food and sanitary towels, we hug those who need them.

Some wounds stay with you for a lifetime. The pain of not knowing what you are going to eat tomorrow kills the passion and vision of becoming better. Together as beautiful and blessed children of God we can make a better tomorrow and inspire young people living with fear and limited resources that delay their plans.

What you donate leaves a smile on the faces of those who are in need and have wounds of not having enough daily. We aim to build a society where every young person will believe that they can achieve whatever they want to achieve, with a mind that is free from limitations and fears.

This charity empowers orphanages to develop self-empowerment and be proud of their beauty, because staying at a shelter does not limit your dreams.
