  • The Woman Behind The Magic

The goal is to build confidence and help those in need.

Life is a rollercoaster,We can do all things through christ who strengthenes us and your confidence is our victory

The pain of not having someone who hugs you when you get home becomes a wound, and together with provision of clothes,food and sanitary towels we hug those who need them.Some wounds stay with you for a life and the pain of not knowing what you're going to eat tomorrow kills the passion and vision of becoming better,together as beautiful and blessed children of God we can make a better tomorrow and inspire young people with fear and who have limited resources that delay their plans. What you donate leaves a smile on the faces of those who are in need and have wounds of not having enough daily.We to build a society where every young person will believe that they can achieve whatever they want to achieve, with a mind that is free from limitations and fears. This charity empowers the orphanages to have self-empowerment and be proud of their beauty because staying at the shelter does not limit your dreams.
