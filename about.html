History

Courage to rise charity was started by a 19-year-old girl with the passion of helping others from Tembisa. At first, it started as an activity from a beauty pageant competition (Miss Teen Gauteng) but as she completed it, she discovered that it’s not just about that only, it’s also about the deep connection with empowerment and giving back. Lucia is a young girl who couldn’t participate in some activities (school competitions and sports), just because she was afraid of the unknown, failure, and judgment. And realizing that most children from townships and rural areas live with that fear and let it make decisions on their behalf, Lucia is becoming a voice that needs to be heard and standing against fear. She accomplishes through educational programs about fear and making donations with the help of the community and MMR bakery.

Mission:

The aim is to build a society where every young person can thrive, free from limitations and fears, and be empowered to achieve their goals.

Vision:

This charity empowers the orphanages to have self-empowerment and be proud of their beauty because staying at the shelter doesn’t limit your ambitions. The most beautiful moments in our lives are those we cherish the most, Keep people who add value to your life and fill your cup with happiness.
